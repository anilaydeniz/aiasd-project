# Week 3 — The screens of your main flow

| # | Screen | What the user does there | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Log in | Enters staff work email address and the 4-digit OTP code sent to it | REQ-001, REQ-006 |
| 2 | Live Inventory Dashboard | Monitors current ingredient stock levels, low-stock warnings, and expiring FIFO batches | REQ-004, REQ-006, REQ-007 |
| 3 | Log Prepared Dishes | Selects prepared recipe, enters completed portion count, and triggers automatic raw ingredient deduction | REQ-002, REQ-006, REQ-007 |
| 4 | Supplier Purchase Order | Reviews auto-compiled replenishment sheet when items fall below safety threshold and confirms dispatch | REQ-003, REQ-006 |
| 5 | Inbound Goods Receiving | Records newly delivered wholesale ingredient crates with quantities and lot expiry dates into inventory | REQ-005, REQ-006 |
