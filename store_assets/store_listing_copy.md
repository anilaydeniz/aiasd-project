(71 karakter)

## Uzun açıklama (≤4000)
KitchenOps helps commercial kitchens keep stock accurate without paperwork.

• Dashboard – see low stock, expiring items and pending orders at a glance
• Live inventory – filter by status, check lots and expiry dates
• Prep – log prepared dishes and deplete ingredients automatically, with undo and a check for unusually large portion counts
• Receiving – import items from a purchase order, scan crate barcodes and add stock to the active inventory
• Waste log – record quantity and reason for spoiled, damaged or canceled items
• Purchase orders – dispatch restock orders when ingredients fall below the safety buffer
• Audit – review movement history
• AI Chat – ask about expiring items, recipes and recent waste. AI answers can be inaccurate; check inventory counts before acting. You can report any answer.

Built for chefs, kitchen managers and receiving staff. Large touch targets and clear contrast for busy kitchens.
